## White Magic
*Associated Skill*: Whitecasting (Willpower)
*Associated Terrain:* Plains

**White Ritualism**
*Activation:* >
Spend 1 Mana to infuse yourself and become the embodiment of the raw power of order. This infusion uses 1 Mana each round.
While infused, you may use Whitecasting in place of Athletics or Agility for any tests you make. In addition, you can use *Truthbind* until the infusion ends.
*\- Truthbind.*  Make a Whitecasting attack against the Spiritual Defense of a creature within Effect Range. On a success, it cannot consciously tell a lie and any oaths they make are Binding. This effect lasts until the end of the infusion.

***Sidebar: Binding Oaths***
A creature Bound by oath cannot consciously break it, under magical compulsion. Binding oaths are permanent, but are void if made by a non-consenting party.

**White Blast**
*Activation:* >>
Infuse 1 or more Mana into target creature within your Effect Range. The infusion uses 1 Mana each round, and for the duration that character recovers health equals to 1d4 + your Whitecasting modifier at the start of each of its turns.
The size of this power’s healing die increases with your ranks in Whitecasting; at 2 ranks, roll 1d6 (instead of 1d4), and so on.
## Blue Magic
*Associated Skill*: Bluecasting (Intellect)
*Associated Terrain:* Island

**Blue Ritualism**
*Activation:* >
To conjure an illusion, spend 1 Mana or more to infuse it into thin air in a space within your Effect Range. This illusion can’t exceed the Effect Size for your ranks in Bluecasting.
Illusions typically take the form of a three-dimensional hologram representing a character, object, or phenomenon you’re familiar with. This illusion is composed of light, complete with animation and accompanying sounds.
*Simple and Complex Illusions.* If the illusion depicts a simple object with no sound or animations, the infusion uses 1 Mana every 10 minutes. If the illusion depicts a character or more complex object, it instead uses 1 Mana each round. For the duration, the illusion remains active even if you move out of range. You can move and control a complex illusion, as detailed in “Controlling Illusions.”
*Deceiving Characters.* If your illusion is a simple object, it automatically convinces characters unless they have a reason to be suspicious. If you create a more complex illusion, the GM might require you to make a Bluecasting test against the Cognitive defense of any character who passively observes the illusion; on a success, your illusion is convincing to them.
A character might decide to Use a Skill to scrutinize your illusion, especially if you fail the above Bluecasting test or if you or your allies roll a Complication on a related test. In this case, make a Bluecasting test opposed by the character’s Perception test. If their test result exceeds your own, they notice the illusion (see “Detecting Illusions” for the effects).
You can make these tests to deceive characters even if that illusion is currently out of your Effect Range.

**Blue Blast**
*Activation:* >>
Spend 1 Mana to make a ranged Bluecasting attack against the Cognitive defense of a creature within Effect Range. On a success, it becomes Surprised.

## Black Magic
*Associated Skill*: Blackcasting (Intellect)
*Associated Terrain:* Swamp

**Black Ritualism**
*Activation:* >
Spend 1 Mana to infuse yourself and channel the spirits that linger around you. This infusion uses 1 Mana each round.
While infused, you become Enhanced \[Intellect]. The bonus to this attribute equals your ranks in Blackcasting. 
In addition, you can use *Séance* until the infusion ends.
*\> Séance.* Deal yourself vital damage equal to your Effect Die in order to temporarily gain an Expertise until the infusion ends.

**Black Blast**
*Activation:* >>
Spend 1 Mana to make a ranged Blackcasting attack against the Spiritual defense of a creature within Effect Range, rolling 2d4 vital damage.
The size of this attack’s damage dice increases with your ranks in Blackcasting; at 2 ranks, roll 2d6 (instead of 2d4), and so on.

## Red Magic
*Associated Skill*: Redcasting (Willpower)
*Associated Terrain:* Mountain

**Red Ritualism**
*Activation:* >
Spend 1 Mana to wield your emotions and cast elemental magic to control, create, or destroy stone, fire, water, or wind.
Unless otherwise stated, the elements you create with ritualism are normal in every way; stone and water persist until they are otherwise destroyed, and fire burns for as long as it has fuel to sustain it.

**Red Blast**
*Activation:* >>
Spend 1 Mana to use magic in an area up to the size you can affect with your ranks in Redcasting. Make one Redcasting attack and compare the test result to the Physical defense of each character of your choice in that area rolling 2d4 energy damage. 
The size of this attack’s damage dice increases with your ranks in Redcasting; at 2 ranks, roll 2d6 (instead of 2d4), and so on.

## Green Magic
*Associated Skill*: Greencasting (Willpower)
*Associated Terrain:* Forest

**Green Ritualism**
*Activation:* >>
You infuse Mana into a living thing other than yourself within Effect Range.
*Flora.* If you target a living plant (or its seed) with your ritualism, spend 1 Mana to cause it to rapidly grow to a size of your choice. This can’t exceed the Effect Size for your ranks in Greencasting.
*Fauna.* If you target a living Animal, infuse 1 or more Mana into it. The infusion uses 1 Mana each round and, for the duration, you can communicate with it as if you share a language and it cannot resist your influence.

***Sidebar: Animals***
Animals, for the purposes of green magic, are creatures with the Animal type. Humanoids are not considered animals.

**Green Blast**
*Activation:* >>
Infuse 1 or more Mana into target creature within your Effect Range. The infusion uses 1 Mana each round, and for the duration that character is Enhanced \[Strength]
or Enhanced \[Speed] (your choice when you start the infusion). The bonus to this attribute equals your ranks in Greencasting.
